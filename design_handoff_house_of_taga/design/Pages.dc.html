the writer

House of Taga is written by Lexi Zotomayor, a journalist who spent years reporting on Saipan — covering its courts, its pension fund, its archaeology and the people who keep its past alive.

The name comes from the House of Taga on Tinian, the great latte stones said to have belonged to the chief Taga. Like those stones, these stories are meant to hold something up: a record of the islands, in their own words where possible.
